### Fixed

- **Price, bag and zone changes no longer plan routes through Shortest Path.** With reagents that vendors sell only a few at a time, each of those changes asked Shortest Path for travel times to their vendors, and the game could freeze or stop with "script ran too long". The shopping list now takes the limit from the nearest of those vendors in a straight line, and the Gear and Buffs pages work out travel times when you click. Thanks to ZMasterKing for the freeze report.
