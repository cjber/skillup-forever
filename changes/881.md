### Fixed

- **A vendor with limited stock no longer prices your whole route.** After you saw a vendor selling a few of a reagent cheaply, such as 4 Peacebloom, the route priced every one it needed at that vendor, even once you had bought them all. The route now counts only what the vendor had in stock when you last looked, none once it is sold out, and plans and prices the rest at the auction house. Thanks to st3nlee on CurseForge for the report (#881).
