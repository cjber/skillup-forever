### Changed

- **Settings have one row for each choice.** Prices on recipe rows is one checkbox with the price it shows beside it, and the route, gear and buffs tabs each keep their own checkbox. Anything you had switched off through the old all-in-one switches stays off.

### Removed

- **Four switches that only repeated other rows are gone.** Show SkillUp tooltip details, Show SkillUp profession tabs and Show cost per skill-up each hid what the rows below them already choose. The Forever addons page and its hints about other addons are gone with them.
